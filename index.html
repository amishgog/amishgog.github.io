Research

Symmetry-aware jet tagging for CMS

Feb – Sep 2026

Institute for Particle Physics and Astrophysics, ETH Zürich. Advisors: Prof. Thea Klaeboe Aarrestad, Patrick Odagiu

A jet's origin doesn't depend on the frame it's viewed in, so a tagger's output shouldn't either. I imposed SO(2), SO(3) and Lorentz invariance on DeepSets, MLP and Transformer taggers, through data augmentation and through a regularisation loss, and compared the effect on performance. I also built the training pipeline in PyTorch Lightning and Hydra.

  • Jet tagging
  • Equivariant ML
  • Transformers
  • PyTorch Lightning

CP violation in B0 and B0s mixing at FCC‑ee

Jul – Sep 2026

High Energy Physics Laboratory, EPFL (S3P3). Advisors: Prof. Laurent Dufour, Dr. Andrea Villa

The Standard Model predicts the semileptonic asymmetry assl to be of order 10−5, so a larger value would signal new physics in B0s mixing. Using FCC‑ee simulation, I designed the analysis in FCCAnalyses from scratch and estimated the precision FCC‑ee could reach on assl, compared with today's world average. Best Talk and Best Poster awards.

  • Flavour physics
  • Future colliders
  • FCCAnalyses

Entanglement detection for two-qubit X-states

Sep 2024 – May 2025

Bachelor thesis, Delhi Technological University. Advisors: Dr. Satyabrata Adhikari, Dr. Mukhtiyar Singh

The standard realignment criterion misses some entangled states. I derived a modified realignment inequality from the singular values and the PPT eigenvalue structure that is necessary and sufficient for two-qubit X-states, written directly in density-matrix elements, and mapped the parameter regions where it catches states the standard test misses.

  • Quantum information

Machine learning for doped SnO2 gas sensors

Dec 2024 – May 2025

Research Assistant, Department of Applied Physics, DTU. Advisor: Dr. Deshraj Meena

Trained random forest, CatBoost, XGBoost, neural-network and stacked models on literature data to predict sensor response, then used them to map how dopant level, temperature and gas concentration drive performance.

  • Gradient boosting
  • scikit-learn

Exotic hadrons in B-meson decays at LHCb

Jun – Jul 2024

High Energy Physics Laboratory, EPFL. Advisors: Prof. Lesya Shchutska, Dr. Yunxuan Song

Searched for exotic hadrons in LHCb Run 1 and Run 2 data. Trained boosted decision trees in TMVA for signal–background separation, extracted yields with 1D and 2D RooFit fits, and ran a partial-wave analysis with TF-PWA.
